This MATH-646 course project extends a mobility-based SIRS epidemic model into a stochastic continuous-time Markov chain framework.

Main components:

- Constructed a stochastic CTMC extension of the deterministic mobility model.
- Derived the law-of-large-numbers ODE limit and central-limit diffusion approximation.
- Studied how mobility heterogeneity affects endemic prevalence, equilibrium noise, and extinction probability.

The main conclusion was that mobility heterogeneity can act as a regularizing mechanism, suppressing endemic noise and increasing extinction probability relative to a homogeneous baseline.

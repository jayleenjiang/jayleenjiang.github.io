This RIPS 2026 project at IPAM/UCLA, sponsored by Analog Devices, studies image correspondence across visible and infrared modalities for factory robot perception.

Main components:

- Evaluation framework for classical and learned feature-matching methods.
- Failure-mode analysis under thermal hotspots, low texture, specular surfaces, and repeated structures.
- Matching pipelines using descriptor confidence, geometric consistency, and abstention criteria.

The applied motivation is safety-critical robotic perception: reducing false correspondences can matter as much as increasing the raw number of matches.

This independent study project models the evolution of costly cooperative behavior in biological systems.

Main components:

- ODE models with reproduction dynamics, cost-benefit tradeoffs, and kin recognition.
- Continuous recognition coefficients that smooth invasion-threshold behavior.
- NetLogo and Python/C++ simulations with genotype tracking, energy budgets, and stochastic reproduction.

The project uses simulation to test when analytical predictions are robust under stochastic individual-level dynamics.

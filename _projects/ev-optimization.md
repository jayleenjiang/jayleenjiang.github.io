This project formulates EV charger placement as a multi-objective optimization problem on a weighted transportation network. The repository includes data processing, CDI model diagnostics, candidate-site ablation, greedy optimization, and validation scripts for manuscript results.

Main components:

- Demand and charging-dependency modeling from demographic and infrastructure data.
- Candidate-site reduction and greedy/local-improvement optimization.
- Greenfield and marginal deployment experiments.
- Reproducibility scripts for tables, figures, and model audits.

The broader aim is to reason about infrastructure placement under competing objectives: access, utilization, and distributional fairness.

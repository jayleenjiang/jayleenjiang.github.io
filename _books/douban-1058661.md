Delicate language with a faint sadness. "The Story of the Fish and the Bird" feels like the quotation bank from which generations of students have drawn love letters. Who has not used some version of "the farthest distance is not between life and death, but between you and me when you do not know I love you"? The words sound romantic, but how many people have truly considered what lies within them?

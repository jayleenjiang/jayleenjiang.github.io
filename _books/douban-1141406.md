In some ways, this is no different from a contemporary romance novel: being short, plain, and poor does not matter, because self-respect and self-love will somehow bring a sudden inheritance and union with one's secret love. But without that inheritance, and without Rochester becoming blind, could they really have lived as equals? As a child, every assigned response focused on dignity and equality. Looking back, that feels almost ridiculous. It is nowhere near as good as Wuthering Heights.

Spring break goal 1/3. The prose has Ishiguro's usual restraint, tenderness, and quiet warmth. By April 16, procrastination had won again. After Klara arrived at Josie's home, the narrative felt rather flat, and I skimmed until slowing down for the ending. After reading Never Let Me Go, it is hard not to approach Ishiguro defensively, knowing how easily a needle can hide inside such gentle storytelling. Yet the ending was simpler than I expected. When Klara's long-foreseen future finally arrives, no greater catastrophe follows. Her worship of the Sun endures, just as the search for the meaning of human loneliness and companionship remains unresolved. If I had a Klara, she would probably watch me transform from a sunny, cheerful girl into a gloomy, sarcastic one.

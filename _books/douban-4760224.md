Yu Hua's prose is always so plain and unadorned, yet it strikes directly at the most fragile place in the heart. Chronicle of a Blood Merchant also exposes the absurdity of an earlier society, but it contains a great deal about human affection, especially the relationship between Xu Sanguan and Yile. I may be too sensitive; after To Live, I could not recover for a week, so I have become afraid of stories that swing between joy and sorrow. The ending is ironic, yet I was genuinely surprised that everyone remained alive and materially secure. I expected Xu Sanguan to die, which would have made it even more absurd. Yu Hua has said that the novel expresses a pursuit of equality, but I did not really see that. What I saw more clearly was criticism and genuine feeling.

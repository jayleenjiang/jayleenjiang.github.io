I finished it in one sitting at a bookstore and left with a heavy mind. Its reputation is deserved. What stayed with me most was the absurdity of the judicial system and the constraints of blind religious morality. I feel somewhat similar to Meursault: lazy, easygoing, and indifferent to certain things. Compared with his passive, almost Zen-like life, though, I still have a little ambition. One suggestion: do not read the introduction first. Its analysis is so complete that, to preserve your own thinking, it is better saved for the end.

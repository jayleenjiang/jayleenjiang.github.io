The quality hardly needs explaining: once again, science fiction at its peak. I do not particularly like Cheng Xin, a saintly heroine who is entrusted with enormous responsibility for no convincing reason, and the final relationship tangle among Cheng Xin, Guan Yifan, AA, and Yun Tianming feels strange. Still, these flaws do not diminish the whole.

The first half, with its marsh setting, suspense, and paranoid outsider heroine, was perfectly tailored to my tastes. Then the romance suddenly took over, and I thought: of course romantic novels have a bigger market. Still, I finally found a huge Goodreads favorite that I could patiently finish.

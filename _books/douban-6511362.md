"As long as a person is alive, they remain capable of changing themselves, even radically; children are not the only ones with the capacity to grow." A brilliant critique of Freud. What resonates most with me now is the way anxiety is sustained by helplessness and resistance to change. Helplessness feels like weakness and incompetence; change means disrupting the present and conflicting with the strong, composed self I imagine I should be. The result is endless self-torment. Change hurts, but anxiety hurts more. Life is full of struggle.

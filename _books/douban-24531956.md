I finished the first book in sixth grade and fell straight down the rabbit hole. I wanted to hide the books in my desk and read during class. Graduation was approaching and life was busy, yet I squeezed in enough time to devour six volumes. To make me prepare for middle-school admission, Dad even hid Deathly Hallows. 😂 I should write a long review someday. P.S. On the first reading, I thought Harry should end up with Hermione. Later I understood that Harry really belonged with Ginny. Now I finally see that Harry and Draco are the true love story. Kidding. Mostly.

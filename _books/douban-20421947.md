I stopped halfway. Unlike To Live and Chronicle of a Blood Merchant, this is not narrated in the first person. Its pace is very slow, like sitting beside a stove in winter and recalling the small details of a former life until they melt into a sigh. Each story stands alone without a fixed structure, simply narrated, yet they quietly complete one another, like a low murmur: "Did you miss that? Let me tell it again." Yu Hua remains my favorite in Chinese literature.

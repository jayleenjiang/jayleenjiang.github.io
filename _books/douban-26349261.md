I happened to read the opening in elementary school, then somehow took more than a decade to finish the book. I could not let it go: wandering, suffering, hardship, luck, hope, love, fate. The warmth carried by the writing wrapped gently around my heart until I was crying with recognition. I thought about my own education, moving from a county town to overseas, living in seven or eight different places, from a three-story house to a ten-square-meter rental room. How lucky I have been to have people who love me beside me throughout it all. Time really can heal every wound. I believe everything will slowly, slowly become better.

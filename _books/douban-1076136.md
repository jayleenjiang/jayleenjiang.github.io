I read this when I was very young, and remembering it still brings a pure kind of sadness. I recall the brothers jumping from the cliff together at the end and entering the world beyond the afterlife. It may be one of the few tragic endings I encountered in children's literature, though in a way it is also open-ended: an infinite cycle.

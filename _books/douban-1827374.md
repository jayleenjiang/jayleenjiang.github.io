I felt thoroughly fooled; the truth remains unreachable until the final page. That is the charm of Agatha Christie's mysteries. Poirot's decision becomes a final struggle between compassion and responsibility. Christie's detectives can soften, worry about ordinary life, and never feel impossibly remote. That is one reason I love them.

In my memory, fairy tales were always beautiful: the prince and princess lived happily ever after. Wilde's tales alone radiate sorrowful beauty. The frozen swallow and the nightingale whose heart is pierced both die beautifully for what they desire, and the stories even carry a satirical edge. Then I understood why Wilde's work is called fairy tales for adults.

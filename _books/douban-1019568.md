I read a young readers' edition in sixth grade and was completely captivated. For the first time, I secretly took a book to the bathroom and read by flashlight, finishing it after midnight. My mother caught me when she got up and gave me a beating. What stayed with me, beyond the physical pain, was the revelation this book gave me: the power of words.

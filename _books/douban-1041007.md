While preparing to choose a middle school, I was so obsessed with Harry Potter that my dad hid the book on the top shelf of the wardrobe. He always used the same hiding place. Clever little me quietly pulled it out, finished it under the covers by flashlight, and put it back. To this day, Dad still thinks my successful school placement was his doing. 😂

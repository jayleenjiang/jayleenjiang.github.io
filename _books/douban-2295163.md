I cannot put it into words. I love Harry Potter so, so much: the original books, fan fiction, films, games, everything. I love every vivid, living character. Even in dreams I can hear owl wings brushing past my ears. My only regret is that no letter arrived when I was eleven. For a while I truly believed the magical world was real and that Rowling must have escaped a Memory Charm by sheer luck, allowing her to create such a vast work of history.

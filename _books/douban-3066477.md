The finest science fiction I have read so far. Every scientific concept is explained clearly and accessibly without ever becoming dry. Many details only click into place much later. No matter how explosively technology advances, what briefly saves humanity in the end is a breakthrough in cosmic theory. Such rigorous thought and formidable imagination: please call me a devoted Cixin Liu fan. P.S. Is Ye Wenjie the real final boss? 🧐

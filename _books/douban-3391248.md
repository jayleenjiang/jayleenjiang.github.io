Life was busy, and I was also distracted by shipping fictional couples, so I stumbled through this book over nearly two weeks. Its vivid and precise language alone makes it worth reading. I cannot say whether writing matters to me as much as the Golden Pavilion does to the narrator, but for one moment I recognized something in its reflections on beauty and art, and in the impulse to destroy what cannot be possessed. Beauty is ultimately intangible and impossible to grasp; once it comes too close and seems touchable, it can instead give birth to wrongdoing.

My first Zijin Chen novel. The prose was, unsurprisingly, not especially impressive. In the end, the plot still relies on the power of state law as an indirect investigative tool, while Yan Liang feels like a blend of Ishigami and Yukawa. I also do not understand why Luo Wen, with such remarkable psychological resilience, would attempt suicide. After searching for his wife and daughter for so long without success, surely he must have had some sense of the truth.

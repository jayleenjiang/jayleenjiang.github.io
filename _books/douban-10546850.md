A concise and accessible introduction to astrology. Being a Capricorn sun and Leo moon feels absurdly divided; I keep swinging between "this is all feudal superstition" and "there really is something to it." I previously studied tarot out of curiosity and learned palmistry on a whim to flirt with girls. The funny thing is that, once you go far enough, different mystical systems start connecting: even Chinese lunar mansions seem to echo Western astrology. Perhaps some things really are written in the stars. At this rate I will eventually become a wandering fortune-teller.

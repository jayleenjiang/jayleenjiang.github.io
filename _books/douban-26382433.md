I fought sleep and finished it in one sitting. My heart felt blocked by cotton, heavy and soft. Between the lines I caught reflections of myself: parents, control, expectations, an older brother, distorted love. Everyone has their own way of living and their own misfortune. Hope is good; without it, what could hold up the weight of a body? Hope is also the worst thing, especially when projected onto the next generation. Then it becomes the bud of disappointment and fertile ground for obsession and madness. Thankfully, my parents pulled me from the lake, and my brother dried my soaked hair.
